## Business rules

| # | Rule | Enforced where | Coverage files |
|---|---|---|---|
| BR01 | Accept PDF/DOCX/PPTX; 1-5 files per batch, at most 5 MB each and 50 MB combined. Validate before processing. | [Browser validation](../frontend/lib/upload-validation.js), [server validation](../backend/app/core/upload_validation.py). | [Backend validation](../backend/tests/test_upload_validation.py), [browser validation](../frontend/tests/upload-validation.spec.js). |
| BR02 | Targets: five contracts within 5 minutes; a single 15-page PDF bullet summary within 3 minutes. Show failures/timeouts. | [Processing worker](../backend/app/services/processing.py) enforces per-attempt timeouts and the PDF bullet deadline. A global 300-second contract-batch deadline is not enforced. [Results](../frontend/pages/results/%5BjobId%5D.js) shows saved failures. | [Timeouts](../backend/tests/test_processing_timeouts.py), [benchmark](../backend/tests/test_t38_benchmark.py), [job states](../frontend/tests/job-lifecycle.spec.js). |
| BR03 | Confidence at least 80% is a high-confidence suggestion; lower values need review. AI labels do not choose folders. User decisions are stored separately. | [Persistence](../backend/app/services/result_persistence.py), [metadata](../frontend/components/DocumentMetadata.js), [label editor](../frontend/components/DocumentLabelEditor.js). | [Labels](../backend/tests/test_user_labels.py), [folder independence](../backend/tests/test_folders.py), [confidence display](../frontend/tests/document-metadata.spec.js). |
| BR04 | Concept overview: 3-5 supported concepts. Summary: at most 1,000 words. Do not pad short sources; explain insufficient support. | [Analysis generation](../backend/app/services/gemini_analysis.py), [output validation](../backend/app/schemas/result.py). | [Concepts](../backend/tests/test_concept_processing.py), [AI output](../backend/tests/test_gemini_analysis.py), [concept display](../frontend/tests/concepts.spec.js). |
| BR05 | Verified amounts, dates, percentages and clauses require document/page evidence. Unsupported claims are Unverified. Open the cited page and highlight when possible. | [Evidence validation](../backend/app/services/evidence.py), [citations](../frontend/components/CitationBadge.js), [reader extraction](../backend/app/services/reader_extraction.py). PDF citations use source pages; DOCX citations use generated reader pages and PPTX citations use slides. | [Evidence](../backend/tests/test_evidence.py), [persisted evidence](../backend/tests/test_evidence_persistence.py), [reader presentation](../backend/tests/test_reader_presentation.py), [citations](../frontend/tests/citations.spec.js). |
| BR06 | Retain ordinary-user documents for 30 days after last access, warn 7 days before expiry and renew after successful open/download/update. | [Retention](../backend/app/services/document_retention.py), [cleanup](../backend/app/services/retention_cleanup.py), [expiry display](../frontend/components/DocumentExpiry.js). | [Renewal](../backend/tests/test_document_retention.py), [cleanup](../backend/tests/test_retention_cleanup.py), [warnings](../frontend/tests/expiry.spec.js). |
| BR07 | Guests access only `/`; protected routes require sign-in and all document/job operations check ownership. | [Route guard](../frontend/proxy.js), [security](../backend/app/core/security.py), [document API](../backend/app/api/document.py), [job API](../backend/app/api/job.py). | [Route guards](../frontend/tests/route-guard.spec.js), [access denial](../frontend/tests/document-access.spec.js), [file access](../backend/tests/test_document_file.py). |
